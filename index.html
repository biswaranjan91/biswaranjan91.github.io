01 / About

Chasing Ghost Particles

I am a Ramanujan Fellow at the Center for High Energy Physics (CHEP), Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore — one of India's most prestigious research fellowships.

My research spans the full spectrum of experimental neutrino physics: from developing cryogenic instrumentation and photon detection systems to precision measurements of neutrino oscillations and interactions. I am a Principal Investigator in the SBND, ICARUS, and DUNE collaborations at Fermilab.

A key focus of my current work is the pioneering development of Power-over-Fiber (PoF) technology — a breakthrough approach to powering electronics in harsh cryogenic environments, enabling next-generation photon detection for low-energy neutrino physics.

Beyond research, I am deeply committed to mentorship and outreach, having guided over 23 students across PhD, MSc, and undergraduate levels across four countries.

Simulation & Analysis Tools

GENIE Neutrino Generator Standard neutrino interaction Monte Carlo generator for HEP experiments
ROOT Framework CERN's data analysis framework — essential for HEP data analysis
LArSoft Liquid argon TPC simulation and reconstruction framework (DUNE, ICARUS, SBND)
NuWro Generator Neutrino event generator — used for νeμτRINO interaction studies
Geant4 Detector simulation toolkit — used in all major neutrino experiments
